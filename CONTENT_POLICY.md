# Content policy

These rules apply to every pack shared through the QWC Remastered pack store, and to packs shared in the project's
own spaces (this repository, its Discord server).

The pack store doesn't host files. Each listing points at a file its author hosts, and every listing is reviewed
before it appears. The launcher installs a pack only if the downloaded file is exactly the one that was reviewed.

## What a pack may contain

- **Your own work:** textures, models, animations, sounds, music, shaders, screen layouts, HUDs, looks and tuning
  that you made.
- **Other people's work, with their permission:** credited in the pack (QWC Studio's **Credits and permissions**).
- **Recipes:** instructions that change the player's own game files on their own computer (crops, filters,
  adjustments), as long as the pack itself carries none of the game's files.

## What a pack must not contain

- **Files from Quidditch World Cup**, or from any other game.
- **Anything made from them:** upscales (by hand or by AI), edits, repaints, redraws, traced or re-created artwork,
  extracted or converted models, animations or sounds, recordings.
- **Logos, names or artwork belonging to Electronic Arts, Warner Bros., J.K. Rowling or their partners.**
- **Anything else you don't have the right to share.**

## Declaring it

Before a pack can be listed, its authors make a statement in QWC Studio (the pack's **For the pack store**
section): that everything in it is their own work or used with permission, whether it uses recipes, the licence it
is shared under, and the credits. The statement is saved in the pack's `pack.ron`. Packs made only for yourself
don't need it.

## Review, reports and removal

- Every version of every pack is reviewed before it is listed, by the project's reviewers: players chosen because
  they want to help. Reviewers don't review their own packs.
- Anyone can report a pack, a collection or a profile with **Report** in the launcher, or on the project's Discord
  server: https://discord.gg/n7btswnbCy
- Listings that break these rules are removed. Accounts that break them repeatedly are closed.

Rights holders can raise a complaint on the Discord server without an account; complaints are acted on quickly.
