The first public release of Quidditch World Cup Remastered: the 2003 PC game decompiled, rebuilt in Rust, and
opened up for modding. It plays from your own copy of the game.

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a very early, work-in-progress build.** Expect bugs, crashes and missing features. Resource packs (the
> remastered textures, models and movies) are **not publicly available yet**: out of the box the game plays as the
> original.

## What you need

- Your own copy of Quidditch World Cup for Windows, installed or copied from the disc.
- Your own no-CD (unwrapped) `QWC.exe`: the remaster cannot read the copy-protected retail one. The launcher checks
  your files during setup and imports them. Nothing of the original game comes with the remaster, and we don't
  distribute it.
- A graphics card with Vulkan, Direct3D 12 or Metal.

## What is in it

- **The launcher:**
  - sets up and checks your game files;
  - manages resource packs;
  - keeps backups of your saves;
  - lists your recent matches;
  - checks for updates;
  - online play: host a match or join one by address, with no servers in between.
- **The game:**
  - the original's matches, House Cup, World Cup and challenges;
  - the remastered lighting with shadows, fog and colour grading per stadium;
  - vector fonts and wide-screen menus;
  - gamepads.
- **Resource packs:**
  - textures (compressed on the GPU), movies, screen layouts, fonts and stadiums;
  - players and props from glTF, on the original skeleton, with their facial morphs;
  - WGSL material shaders and post passes;
  - for Gameplay packs, tuning of the game's console variables.
- **QWC Studio:** makes and edits packs. It can:
  - export the game's textures and models to edit;
  - check models against the original's skeleton;
  - edit tuning and shaders, with checks as you type;
  - build `.qwcpack` files.

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `QWCRemastered-0.1.0-setup.exe`. It installs for your user only, with no administrator needed,
  and offers a desktop shortcut and Start menu entries.
- **Linux:** run the AppImage, or unpack the tarball and run `qwc-launcher`.

Your library (the imported game, your packs and settings) stays in your user profile when you update or uninstall.
`SHA256SUMS` lists each file's checksum.
