Setting up your game is now part of the Windows installation, and the launcher shows release notes in full.

> [!WARNING]
> **This is still a very early, work-in-progress build.** Resource packs are not publicly available yet: out of
> the box the game plays as the original.

## What's new

- **Setup during installation (Windows):** the installer asks for your game folder and your no-CD `QWC.exe`, checks
  both when you click Next, and imports your game files once the programs are installed.
  - You can choose to set it up later, in the launcher.
  - Updating over a version already set up keeps your game as it is, unless you ask to set it up again.
- **Release notes in the launcher:** the Latest update card shows the whole notes and scrolls when they are longer
  than the card. The News page lays them out properly: headings, lists and warnings, with no lines missing.
- **The logo everywhere:** the launcher, the game and QWC Studio carry the logo as their icon, in Explorer, on the
  taskbar and on the game's window.
- **Shortcuts are your choice:** the installer offers the Start menu entries and the desktop shortcut as options.

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `QWCRemastered-0.1.1-setup.exe`, or update from the launcher's News page.
- **Linux:** run the AppImage, or unpack the tarball and run `qwc-launcher`.

Your library (the imported game, your packs and settings) stays in your user profile when you update or uninstall.
`SHA256SUMS` lists each file's checksum.
