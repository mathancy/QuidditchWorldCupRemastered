The pack store arrives: browse and install packs other players made, share your own, and put collections together.

> [!WARNING]
> **This is still a very early, work-in-progress build.** The store is brand new, so it starts empty: packs appear as
> their authors submit them and reviewers approve them.

## What's new

- **The pack store** (Packs, Store):
  - browse and search packs, newest, best rated, most installed or by name;
  - Install downloads a pack from its author's own link and installs it only if it is exactly the file that was
    reviewed (its size and SHA-256), so a pack can't change behind its listing.
- **Collections:** packs chosen and ordered to work together.
  - Install one in one go; every pack in it is checked the same way, and your own order is kept as a saved order
    to go back to.
  - Share your own order as a collection.
- **Accounts, with Discord:**
  - the Play page's bottom strip has your picture and name, and Log in and Log out;
  - profiles show the packs and collections a player shares;
  - choose your store name, whether your Discord username shows, and your picture (your Discord avatar, or upload
    your own);
  - the store asks Discord for nothing but your id, name and avatar, and keeps your Discord details encrypted.
- **Sharing your packs:** QWC Studio's For the pack store records that a pack is your own work, its licence and
  credits; Packs, Your packs, Submit to the store checks your download link gives exactly your file, then sends it
  for review. Your profile shows where each submission stands.
- **Ratings and reports:** rate the packs you installed from the store, and report anything that breaks the
  [content rules](https://github.com/mathancy/QuidditchWorldCupRemastered/blob/main/CONTENT_POLICY.md).
- **Saved orders:** keep an order of packs under a name and come back to it.
- **For reviewers:** a Review page to check, try, approve or reject submissions and act on reports.

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `QWCRemastered-0.1.2-setup.exe`, or update from the launcher's News page.
- **Linux:** run the AppImage, or unpack the tarball and run `qwc-launcher`.

Your library (the imported game, your packs and settings) stays in your user profile when you update or uninstall.
`SHA256SUMS` lists each file's checksum.
