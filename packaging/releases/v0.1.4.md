Share your packs from OneDrive and Google Drive, and large packs download on slow connections.

> [!NOTE]
> This is still a very early, work-in-progress build.

## What's new

- **Packs shared from OneDrive and Google Drive:** Submit to the store takes a file's share link as it is (personal
  OneDrive, work or school OneDrive and SharePoint, or a Google Drive file's link, shared with anyone who has the
  link). The launcher downloads the file itself from it. A link that gives a web page instead (a sign-in, or Google
  Drive's notice that a file was downloaded too often today) is refused as such.
- **Large downloads finish on slow connections:** the launcher stopped any download that took longer than 30
  seconds, so a large pack failed to install, or to check when it was submitted, unless the connection was fast.
  Now a download takes as long as it needs, and only one that stops for a minute is given up.
- **For pack authors:** `qwc-launcher check-link LINK` downloads a link as installs do and prints the file's size and
  SHA-256, to check it before you submit.
- **For reviewers:** the store's admin may review their own packs; each such review is marked as one.

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `QWCRemastered-0.1.4-setup.exe`, or update from the launcher's News page.
- **Linux:** run the AppImage, or unpack the tarball and run `qwc-launcher`.

Your library (the imported game, your packs and settings) stays in your user profile when you update or uninstall.
`SHA256SUMS` lists each file's checksum.
